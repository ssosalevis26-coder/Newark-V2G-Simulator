How to read the money

PJM Regulation pays for available power to help balance the grid. This historical benchmark subtracts incremental charging losses, aggregator fees, throughput-based battery wear, maintenance allowance, and annualized selected capital. Normal driving energy and bus purchase cost belong to the same-electric-bus baseline and are excluded. Taxes, incentives, financing fees, and unquoted site costs are excluded. A zero maintenance allowance means no cost modeled.

The physical peak-event scenario has no modeled payment or energy savings. Do not add it or the separate Delmarva TOU comparator to annual regulation value. Annual regulation does not simulate route schedules, battery charge trajectories, qualification, or performance scoring. The AC manufacturing increment is not a complete commercial system price.

Model limits and interpretation

    The PJM scenario uses editable weekly market hours and the shared connected-bus count. Both scenarios require compatible hardware and interconnection; market qualification is unverified. The two outputs are not added together.
